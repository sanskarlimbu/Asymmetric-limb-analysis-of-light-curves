# Asymmetric-limb-analysis

This project focuses on the analysis of time series, light curve data of stars that contain transits caused by known exoplanets. 

The ingress and egress limbs of the transit may have asymmetry due to atmospheric temperature discrepancies. Herein lies the main motivation of this project, that the temperature gradient will cause the exoplanet to bulge towards the hotter side and that a single conflated radius obtained through transit photometry doesn't describe the exoplanet as well as it could. A method of fitting a spliced transit curve, limited to as much as either the ingress or egress portion of the transit as possible, was explored making use of the [batman library](https://lkreidberg.github.io/batman/docs/html/index.html#batman-br-bad-ass-transit-model-calculation) to fit a proposed radius for each side of the exoplanet, thus giving a more accurate picture of the planet. Additionally, analysis of the spectrum of fitted radii across light frequencies, was seen to allude to different atmospheric makeups from the hotter and colder sections of the planet.
